Simplified two-mass quarter-car model. Damper stiffness is an optional linear elastic term; real dampers are usually characterised by force–velocity curves. The model omits full double-wishbone kinematics, chassis roll, bump stops, nonlinear parts and one-sided tyre contact. Use it for learning and comparison, not design sign-off or as a substitute for ANSYS and validation.
